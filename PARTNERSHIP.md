# Community Partnership Program

Welcome! The **Open EDA AppImage** project is dedicated to removing installation friction for the open-source EDA / silicon community. By packaging tools like Yosys, ABC, OpenSTA, Surelog, and Netgen into portable, single-file AppImages, we aim to make open-source EDA accessible to everyone, regardless of their Linux distribution or system configuration.

We are actively seeking **Community Partners** and **Ecosystem Supporters** who share our mission of democratizing access to open-source hardware design.

## What is a Community Partnership?
This is a **100% non-financial, non-binding, goodwill recognition** program. We are not asking for funding, code contributions, or technical support.

### What We Offer Partners:
- **Logo & Link Placement:** Your organization's logo and a link to your website will be featured in the "Community Partners" section of our main `README.md`.
- **Shared Mission Visibility:** Public alignment with a grassroots effort to lower the barrier to entry for open-source EDA.

### What We Ask of Partners:
- **Permission:** Explicit consent (via GitHub discussion) to display your organization's name and logo on this repository.

## Trademark & Legal Safety
- **No Endorsement Implied:** This partnership does not imply that the partner organization officially endorses, maintains, or is liable for the AppImages distributed here.
- **Trademark Ownership:** All trademarks, logos, and brand names remain the sole property of their respective owners.
- **Easy Opt-Out:** Partners may request the immediate removal of their name and logo from this repository at any time, for any reason, with no questions asked. We will comply within 24 hours.

## How to Join
If your organization or community group aligns with our mission, we would be honored to have you as a supporter!

1. Reach out to us via GitHub Discussions.
2. Let us know you are interested in the Community Partnership program.
3. We will send you a mock-up of how your logo will appear in the README for your final approval before anything is published.

Thank you for supporting open-source silicon / EDA!
